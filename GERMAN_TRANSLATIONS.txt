Notes on betting terminology used:
- "Quote" is the standard word German bettors/bookmakers use for "Odds".
- "Buchmacher" is the precise bettor term for "Book" / bookmaker (column header).
- "RTP (Auszahlungsquote)" — German bettors recognize RTP; "Auszahlungsquote"
  (payout ratio) is the established native equivalent.
- "Marge des Buchmachers" is the bettor term for the built-in margin / overround.
- "Event" is commonly used by German bettors alongside "Ereignis"; kept as "Event".
- Placeholder examples localized ("Über/Unter" = Over/Under, "usw." = etc.).
- Apostrophes shown plain here; in strings.xml they must be escaped as \' .
