WHAT IS RTP?

Return to Player (RTP) is the share of the money a betting market gives back to players. It is the flip side of the bookmaker's margin: the higher the RTP, the greater the chance of finding an attractive price.

WHY USE RTP CALCULATOR?

A high RTP means bookmakers are taking a smaller margin on the market: the closer the RTP gets to 100%, the better the chances of finding a genuinely attractive price because it is mispriced (what the jargon calls a value bet).

Checking the RTP before placing a bet is therefore a fundamental discipline for any serious bettor, and for anyone looking to protect their bankroll.
