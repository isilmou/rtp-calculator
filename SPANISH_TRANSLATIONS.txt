Notes on betting terminology used:
- "Cuota" is the standard word Spanish bettors/bookmakers use for "Odds" (not "probabilidades").
- "Casa" (short for "casa de apuestas") is how Spanish bettors refer to a bookmaker / "Book".
- "RTP (Retorno al Jugador)" — RTP is widely recognized in Spanish gambling; "Retorno al Jugador" is the established expansion.
- "Margen de la casa" is the bettor term for the bookmaker's built-in margin/overround.
- Placeholder examples localized to markets Spanish bettors know ("Más/Menos" = Over/Under).
- Apostrophes shown plain here; in strings.xml they must be escaped as \' .
