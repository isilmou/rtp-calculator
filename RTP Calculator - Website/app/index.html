How is the RTP calculated?

The RTP (return to player) of a bet is the inverse of the sum of the implied probabilities of the odds. For each outcome, the implied probability is 1 divided by the decimal odds. Add these probabilities up, then divide 1 by the total. Example on a 1X2 market at 2.10 / 3.40 / 3.60: the implied probabilities are 47.6% + 29.4% + 27.8% = 104.8%, so the RTP is 95.4%. The remaining 4.6% is the bookmaker's margin.

How to read the result?

The closer the RTP is to 100%, the less margin the bookmaker takes on that market. An RTP of 95% means that, over the long run, 95 of every 100 staked go back to players. Some regulators cap it: in France, for instance, the ANJ limits the average RTP of sports betting to 85%. It also varies widely from one market and one operator to another, which is exactly what this calculator lets you compare.
