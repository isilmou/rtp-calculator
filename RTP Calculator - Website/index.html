An app available in your browser and on mobile

RTP Calculator comes in two free, no sign-up versions with the same features:

Browser version

Enter the odds of a bet and get its RTP instantly. Supports decimal (EU), fractional (UK) and American (US) odds formats. You can enter an unlimited number of outcomes. Your history is kept in your browser.

Open the calculator (browser version)

Android app

The same features, except that your analyses are saved locally on your smartphone. As in the browser version, you can share your analyses as images.

Download the Android version

Who is it for? Sports bettors who want to know what a bet really gives back and compare odds across operators. RTP Calculator is a decision-support tool — it does not provide betting tips and does not encourage gambling.
