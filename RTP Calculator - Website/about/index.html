About

Why this site exists

Bookmakers offer odds but never display what those odds actually give back to players.

That share is called the RTP, the Return To Player. It can be computed from the odds themselves, in three operations, yet it remains invisible to the vast majority of bettors. The consequence: many bettors amplify their losses by staking on bets with a very unfavourable RTP.

RTP Calculator was built to serve one very simple function: computing the Return To Player rate of sports bets, so that every bettor can act knowingly.

Who publishes this site

This site and the associated Android app are designed, developed and funded by an independent developer operating as a sole trader.

The site is not affiliated with any betting operator.

How we calculate

Every figure published on this site and produced by the app rests on a single formula, verifiable by anyone: each odd is converted into an implied probability, these probabilities are added up, and the RTP follows from the total. It is detailed in the guide The RTP of a sports bet: definition and calculation. The app and the online calculator apply exactly the same logic, with no intermediate rounding.

The odds used in the examples are illustrative: they demonstrate a method, they do not represent any operator's current offer.

What this site does not do

Neither the site nor the app aims to publish betting tips or tipster content.

Concretely:

  • No tips, no selections, no betting advice on this site.
  • No bookmaker rankings, no operator comparisons. We explain a comparison method; you apply it to the operators of your choice.
  • No affiliate links, no commercial partnership with a betting operator. No payment depends on you signing up anywhere.
  • No promise of profit. Knowing a bet's RTP does not make it a winner: it only tells you what it costs.

How the project is funded

The Android app is free and displays a single banner ad. The online calculator is free too and requires no sign-up. Banner ads, kept as unobtrusive as possible, may in time be added there as well to cover the project's infrastructure costs (domain, hosting, etc.).

Your data

The online calculator stores your analyses in your browser only: they are never sent to a server. The Android app keeps your history locally on your phone. Audience measurement is enabled only with your consent, which you can change at any time. The details are in our privacy policy.

Updates

The guides are reviewed and expanded regularly, and new articles are published over time. Every guide shows its publication and update dates.
